Why these 20. Google has indexed 10 of 694 pages and the rest are "discovered, not crawled", so links to the pages that earn money do double duty: they send crawlers to the right pages and add authority. All 20 keywords have KD 29 or lower, every landing page exists and is live, and the mix covers whisky, spirits, wine, champagne and gifts so one link campaign does not depend on a single category.

How to use them (anchor mix and sources)

  • Anchor mix per page: about 40% brand or naked URL ("Doctors of Whisky", doctorsofwhisky.com.au), 30% partial or other anchors from the column above, 20% exact match from the table, 10% generic ("this shop", "here"). Never point many links with the same exact anchor at one page in the same week.
  • Where links should come from: Australian drinks and cocktail blogs, gift guides and Christmas round-ups, local Sydney business directories and Google Business Profile, supplier and brand pages that list stockists, and guest posts or interviews. Prefer editorial links in relevant articles over directories; avoid paid link networks and comment spam, which can bring a manual penalty.
  • Linkable assets for outreach (guides earn links more easily than product pages): vermouth (9,900), sour puss (8,100), pinot gris (5,400), white rum (4,400), limoncello spritz recipe (3,600).
  • Pace: a few new referring domains a week looks natural. Pair each new link with a Search Console indexing request for the landing page (see docs/indexing-queue.md).
  • Champagne first: it is the only cluster already showing in Google (queries such as buy champagne online, champagne online and cheap champagne online). Links to the champagne collection with "buy champagne online" as one anchor are the quickest test of whether off-page work lifts rankings.

What to measure

In Search Console, Performance, Pages: impressions, clicks and average position for each landing page above, weekly. Baseline on 30 Oct: 2 clicks, 1,360 impressions, average position 41.8 over 28 days. A page is working when its average position falls below 20 and clicks start to appear.
