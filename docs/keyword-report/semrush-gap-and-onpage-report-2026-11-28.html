6. Proposed plan (nothing started, awaiting your approval)

  1. Map the "we sell it" gap (4a) to the right pages: new keyword hubs for soju (22,200), fireball, jagermeister, malibu, chartreuse, triple sec / orange liqueur, port, bacardi and others; and add Mumm, Chandon, Bollinger, Wild Turkey, Woodford Reserve, Hibiki and Glenlivet head terms to their hubs.
  2. Re-check each keyword with a fresh Semrush "Update metrics" before it goes into the map; drop those over KD 29.
  3. Add products for the best stocked-gap brands (4b) that fit a spirits and wine shop: Canadian Club, Frangelico, Disaronno, Southern Comfort, Roku gin, Drambuie, St Germain, Pisco, Angostura bitters, Hendrick's, plus moscato and rose wine ranges.
  4. Product pages: turn the 345 name-only primaries into "buy <product name>" flagged fallbacks, and add brand hub links where hubs exist.
  5. Blogs and pagination: fix the blog titles and H1s that miss the primary (about 8 posts); leave paginated pages as they are.
  6. Ranking work (4c): indexing queue and backlinks for the 347 mapped keywords where competitors already rank.

Method notes: gap classification matches each keyword against our stored map (primaries, secondaries, tags, blogs, hubs) and against product names; a product match can be a near-miss, so 4a and 4b need a human check before mapping. Competitor positions are Semrush's, not Google's live results.
