3. What ebikedirect.eu does that DOW does not yet

  1. Model-line pages: one page per product family (e.g. "macallan 12", "glenfiddich 12") to hold big model keywords.
  2. "buy <name>" fallback on products with no bank keyword (Transactional, flagged), instead of the plain name.
  3. Scripted bank check (stored volume/KD/intent must equal the bank row; guard words blocked) run before every push.

4. The rules (Doctors of Whisky golden rule, 2026-10)

Keywords
  • One primary per URL, one keyword = one URL (site-wide unique). Transactional first; Commercial allowed when it has at least 2.5x the volume; brand head terms may be C/N/I when the volume is very high (owner exception).
  • KD 29 or lower. Intent priority T > C > N > I. Volume under 10 is out.
  • Secondaries: 15 stored, 5 visible, all Transactional or Commercial. Tags: 20 stored, 10 visible. Visible copy = 1 primary + 5 secondary + 4 supporting. No stuffing.
  • Homepage primary "whisky sale". About and Contact are brand pages. Blogs: Informational/Navigational primary; the keyword is in the H1.
  • Brand head terms go to the brand hub or collection that sells them, never to a product or a blog.
  • Stock check: a keyword gets a page only if the shop sells it. Guard words ("near me", "review", competitor retailers) are never used.
On-page (every page)
  • Title 60 characters or fewer and starts with the primary; meta 110-158 with the primary and a call to action.
  • Exactly one H1, the first heading in the document, containing the primary; 3+ H2s (blogs 8+); no skipped levels.
  • Primary in the first paragraph; unique copy (no shared blurb across products); truthful claims only.
  • 3+ contextual internal links, varied anchors; outbound authority links (blogs 2+); no competitor retailers; no dead links.
  • Schema: BreadcrumbList; Product + Offer (with shipping delivery time and return policy); FAQPage (5 FAQs, each with one supporting keyword); BlogPosting.
Blogs
  • 1,500+ words, table of contents, visible updated date, 5 FAQs, 3+ authority links, 8+ internal links, unique colour cover (16:9), one topic per post (merge overlaps).
Images
  • Products: real photo on pure white, square 1200 master, no watermark or logo, new filename for every change, alt = the focus keyword.
Process
  • Live crawl, not stored data. Run type-check, build and all audits before every push; re-run on production after deploy; report numbers and what was not verified.
