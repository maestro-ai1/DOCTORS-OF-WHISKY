Does it follow the rules and lead to more buying actions?

  • Rules: every page has one unique Transactional primary (blogs Informational), Commercial secondaries first, KD ≤ 29, no duplicate primaries across pages, 20 tags kept as tag links, related-search chips cut from 15 to 5 to avoid stuffing.
  • Sales intent: the pages that Google can show now carry explicit buying wording (sale, buy, price) in title, H1 and CTA buttons, and the menu puts the collections one click from every page.
  • Caveat: I can’t measure sales lift from here. GSC showed 7 of 689 URLs known to Google on 2026-10-11, so ranking and conversions depend on indexing first.
