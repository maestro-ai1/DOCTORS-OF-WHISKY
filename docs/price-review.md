## Pricing rule

- **Market price M** = median of the verified retailer prices for the same product, size and age (exact match only).
- **Reasonable band** = 0.90 x M to 1.20 x M. Inside the band the price is kept.
- **Too high** (above 1.20 x M) or **too low** (below 0.90 x M): set to M x 1.08, rounded up to .99, and never above 1.10 x the highest verified retailer price.
- **Held:** bottles at $1,000+ that are at most 2.5x the retailer price are kept (allocated bottles carry a secondary-market premium).
- **Not changed** when the two retailers disagree by more than 50% or no exact match exists.
